# Market Relevance Evidence Plan

The ISO/IEC proposal must demonstrate an international need, not merely technical novelty. Saga records evidence in seven categories:

- user or educator surveys;
- deployed case studies;
- procurement or platform requirements;
- education adoption;
- industry support letters;
- privacy-respecting aggregate usage metrics;
- peer-reviewed or institutional research.

Minimum project readiness threshold:

- at least three evidence records;
- evidence from at least two countries;
- verified adoption by at least three organizations in at least three countries;
- a written comparison explaining why an international standard is preferable to a project-only specification.

These thresholds are project gates, not ISO rules and do not guarantee approval.
