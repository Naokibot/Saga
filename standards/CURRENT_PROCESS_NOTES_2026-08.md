# Current standards-process notes used by Saga 0.15

Checked: 2026-08-09.

The project registry model was compared with the current consolidated ISO/IEC Directives, Part 1 proposal-stage clauses 2.3.2–2.3.5 and Annex C. The engineering interpretation used here is:

- eligible NP proposers include a National Body, relevant committee secretariat, another committee, Category A liaison, technical management board/advisory group, or Chief Executive Officer;
- the proposer nominates a Project Leader and should supply a first working draft or at least an outline;
- justification should establish market relevance and enable informed National Body voting;
- NP acceptance requires a two-thirds majority of P-members voting (abstentions excluded) and active participation by at least 4 P-members for committees with 16 or fewer P-members, otherwise at least 5, subject to documented exceptions/increases under the Directives.

The Saga registry only records evidence and computes these checks. The authoritative rules, ballots and decisions remain those of the applicable standards organization at the time of submission.
